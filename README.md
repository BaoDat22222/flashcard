## Advanced in Use 3 - Advanced in Use

### senior
**Context**: byconduct
**Meaning 1**: A person of greater age, rank, or experience than another
- **Synonyms**: elder, superior
- **Example**: He is a senior manager at the company with over 20 years of experience.

**Meaning 2**: A student in the final year of study at a school or college
- **Synonyms**: upperclassman, final-year student
- **Example**: She is a senior in high school and will graduate next spring.
